## The situation

The scout desk (`GET /api/scout/players`) is about to grow an "Introduce" button. The contact rail only lets a
verified scout introduce themselves to a player who has an APPROVED claim of type `player`
(`player_profile_claims` rows with `relationship_type == "player"` and `status == "approved"`). Scout rows carry
no claim information, so the button could not know whom to show. Add a boolean `contactable` to every row of
that list response, computed with ONE batched query (never a per-row lookup).
