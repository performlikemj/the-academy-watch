## The situation

PR review (codex): (1) P1 — the footage redirect always minted a fresh 30-minute SAS, so a link captured near the end of
the 30-minute media token outlived the token by up to 30 minutes; now the SAS is capped at the token's REMAINING life
(`media_token_remaining_seconds` in auth.py; `mint_media_read_sas(..., seconds=…)`). (2) P1 — when blob storage was not
configured, the sweeper still marked rows expired and erased the blob path, stranding footage in Azure; now that counts
as a failed delete and the row is left for a configured run. (3) P2 — `preflight` (an in-flight state) was in the
expirable set; it is excluded like queued/processing. Tests updated.
