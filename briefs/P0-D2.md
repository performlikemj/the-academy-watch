## The situation

Admin reviewers stream a match in the browser through `GET /api/admin/video/matches/<id>/footage?token=…`.
The `token` is a media token that lives **30 minutes** (`MEDIA_TOKEN_TTL = 60 * 30` in `src/auth.py`).
In production that route answers with a 302 to an Azure Blob read-SAS URL minted for **6 hours**
(`READ_SAS_HOURS = 6`, used by the worker's multi-hour footage pull) — so anyone who captures the redirect
target keeps hours of access to youth footage after the token died, and the redirect carries no
`Cache-Control`. Mint a separate, short read SAS for the browser path and mark the redirect `no-store`.
The worker's 6-hour SAS stays exactly as it is.
