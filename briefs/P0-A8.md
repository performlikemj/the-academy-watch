## The situation

When a scout asks to contact a contracted player whose club is on the platform, the club's manager must
grant or decline consent before messaging opens. Managers get an email link (P0-A1/A3) — but inside the
club console there is nothing. Add an "Introductions" tab that lists the club's requests
(`GET /api/contact/requests?box=club`), lets a manager **Allow** / **Decline** while
`club_consent_status === "pending"` (`POST /api/contact/requests/<id>/club-consent` with
`{ action: "grant"|"decline", note }`), and opens the shared `ContactThread` for a selected request
(the club can message once the thread is open). The `contact_request` shape is in `briefs/P0-A6b.md`.
