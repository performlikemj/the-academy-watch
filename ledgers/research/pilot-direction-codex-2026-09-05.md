## Verdict

- **Choose clubs first: Forest contact → one staff-led pilot → adult players → one invited external scout.**
- Sell a **weekly player-review service**, with Film Room operated by the founder and decisions reviewed by the coach.
- The warm lead justifies the first conversation; it does not establish demand or willingness to pay.
- The orchestrator’s direction is right. Its pitch bundles too much, and its proposed build misses local-player contact routing and result correction.
- **Reach out before building invitations or feedback.** First secure a named coach, authorized footage, a review date, and agreement to discuss paid continuation.
- Start the player-facing cohort with verified adults. The current code does not support a complete U18 participation-and-consent journey.
- The smallest software scope is cohort measurement, accepted club relationships with correct contact routing, private player feedback, and correctable results.
- Billing activation can wait for this pilot; the owner’s GOL go-live checklist remains a separate revenue track.
- The refreshed score is **65.1%**. Pursue paid repeat use; the broader rubric also includes funding and ownership beyond this pilot. (`ledgers/tooling/dream-scorecard/scorecard.json:2`; `ledgers/GRADING_dream-scorecard-2026-09-02.md:7`)

## Disagreements with the orchestrator

1. **“Clubs first” is right; the opening pitch is too broad.** Lead with one coach’s recurring review problem. Academy tracking, film analysis and recruitment exposure are separate value propositions with different proof requirements.
