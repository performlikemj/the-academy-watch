# Hugging Face models/datasets for Film Room scouting

Research date: 2026-09-01. “Commercial OK” is a screening result, not legal advice. Dates are the Hugging Face `lastModified` date. **YES\*** means the published model/data licence is permissive, but third-party training/source-media provenance still deserves a release review. `UNVERIFIED` means exactly that.

## Decision

The near-term production path should stay modular: **Qwen3-VL reads/captions; the existing tracked-player box constrains every prompt; a grounding model returns evidence time/location; deterministic code rejects claims whose evidence does not intersect that player’s track.** Do not ask one VLM to identify, observe, merge and write in one pass.

## VLM family findings and Apple LoRA matrix

- **Qwen3-VL:** official Instruct/Thinking sizes **2B, 4B, 8B, 30B-A3B, 32B, 235B-A22B are all Apache-2.0**. Instruct HF updates: 2B 2025-10-23; 4B/8B 2025-10-15; 30B-A3B/235B 2025-11-26; 32B 2025-10-21. It supports image, multi-image and video; model card explicitly claims second-level temporal indexing, timestamp-grounded event localization and 2D/3D grounding. Ollama has 2/4/8/30/32/235 tags but exposes Text+Image, so use extracted frames there; [`mlx-vlm`](https://github.com/Blaizzy/mlx-vlm) has native Qwen video processing. **LoRA/QLoRA: YES and explicitly documented**; start with 4B or 8B 4-bit, language layers only, on corrected observation/caption examples.
- **Qwen2.5-VL:** 3B (2025-04-06) is **Qwen Research / non-commercial: KILL**; 7B (2025-04-06) and 32B (2025-04-14) are Apache-2.0; 72B (2025-06-06) HF field `other`, Qwen licence permits commercial use below 100M MAU. All support long video/event segments and image boxes/points. Ollama + MLX mirrors exist. **LoRA: YES**, but Qwen3-VL dominates the clean-licence local choice.
- **InternVL3 / 3.5:** video/multi-image and visual grounding are present, but no comparable native temporal-output contract and reports of 2D grounding drift make them secondary. InternVL3 1/2/8/14/38B are Apache-2.0, 9B MIT, 78B `other` (updates 2025-05-29 to 2025-09-11); InternVL3.5 1/2/4/8/14/38/241B-A28B are Apache-2.0 (all 2025-08-29). MLX mirrors exist; no official Ollama library family found. **LoRA: supported by mlx-vlm’s all-supported-model rule, but run a one-batch smoke test before committing data.**
- **Molmo2 / MolmoPoint:** 4B/8B general models (2026-01-23) and video-point specialists (2025-12-16 to 2026-03-30) are Apache-2.0 and uniquely return `(timestamp, object_id, x, y)` points/tracks. MLX mirrors exist. However, every relevant card warns that training included third-party academic/non-commercial datasets: excellent **internal judge**, not production-safe without rights clearance. Special-token pointing LoRA in MLX is **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Gemma:** Gemma 3 vision is 4/12/27B under the custom `gemma` licence (2025-03-21); Gemma 4 31B is Apache-2.0 (2026-07-20). Both are useful generic image/multi-image writers, and current mlx-vlm adds Gemma 4 video, but neither supplies the time+box evidence contract needed here. Ollama has Gemma 3 vision; MLX mirrors exist. **LoRA: YES for supported Gemma 3/4; Gemma3n is an explicit mlx-vlm exception.**
- **Other video VLMs:** LLaVA-Video 7B (Apache-2.0, 2024-10-25) and Apollo 1.5/3/7B mirrors (Apache-2.0, 2024-12-18) are video-native but not spatially grounded and CUDA-centric. 2026 [`Marlin-2B`](https://huggingface.co/lunahr/Marlin-2B-ungated) (Apache-2.0, 2026-05-22) and [`microsoft/Mage-VL`](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Mage-VL) (Apache-2.0, 2026-08-10) are interesting timestamp/dense-caption watchlist models, but neither returns player boxes; Mage’s MLX video path was still unimplemented as of July 2026. [`KlingTeam/TimePLE`](https://huggingface.co/KlingTeam/TimePLE) (Apache-2.0, 2026-08-15) is another temporal-only 8B challenger.
- **Practical Apple fine-tune choice:** [`mlx-vlm` LoRA docs](https://github.com/Blaizzy/mlx-vlm/blob/main/mlx_vlm/LORA.MD) say LoRA/QLoRA works for all supported models except Gemma3n and Qwen3 Omni and give a Qwen3-VL example. Use Qwen3-VL-4B/8B 4-bit, `--train-on-completions`, frozen vision tower first; keep an untouched evaluation set of clips/players/venues. VideoLoom, TimeLens specialized heads, Florence-2 and MolmoPoint pointing fine-tunes on MLX are **UNVERIFIED** even where inference/checkpoint conversion exists.

Exact official HF IDs checked: `Qwen/Qwen3-VL-{2B,4B,8B,32B}-Instruct`, `Qwen/Qwen3-VL-30B-A3B-Instruct`, `Qwen/Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B-Instruct`; `Qwen/Qwen2.5-VL-{3B,7B,32B,72B}-Instruct`; `OpenGVLab/InternVL3-{1B,2B,8B,9B,14B,38B,78B}`; `OpenGVLab/InternVL3_5-{1B,2B,4B,8B,14B,38B,241B-A28B}`; `allenai/Molmo2-{4B,8B}`, `allenai/Molmo2-VideoPoint-4B`, `allenai/MolmoPoint-{8B,Vid-4B}`; `google/gemma-3-{4b,12b,27b}-it`, `google/gemma-4-31B-it`; `lmms-lab/LLaVA-Video-7B-Qwen2`; `GoodiesHere/Apollo-LMMs-Apollo-7B-t32`; `microsoft/Florence-2-{base,large}`. Where an ID’s HF metadata did not expose a standard licence, the report preserves `other` or `UNVERIFIED` rather than normalizing it.

## Jersey / re-ID / pitch / pose notes

- **Jersey:** PARSeq tiny/small weights are Apache-2.0; the official code is Apache-2.0. SoccerNet Jersey has the right task—2,853 low-resolution player tracklets with `-1` for not visible—but all SoccerNet data is research-only. New HF models [`Akashpaul123/jersey-number-recognition-temporal`](https://huggingface.co/Akashpaul123/jersey-number-recognition-temporal) (Apache-2.0, 2025-12-11) and [`prxkc/jersey-number-recognition`](https://huggingface.co/prxkc/jersey-number-recognition) (MIT, 2026-02-02) are tiny temporal baselines, but their custom training-data licence/provenance is **UNVERIFIED**. E-BARD’s 16-frame basketball stubs are CC-BY-4.0, but NBA-footage rights and soccer transfer make it eval-only.
- **Re-ID:** OSNet is the first benchmark; [`occurra/person_vit_clip_reid`](https://huggingface.co/occurra/person_vit_clip_reid) is an MIT ONNX/PyTorch CLIP-ReID port (2026-05-27) worth a second challenger. SoccerNet-ReID models/data are broadcast-domain and research-only. DINOv3 HF fields are `other`; use Apache DINOv2 instead. Train final merge scoring on the product’s own human accept/reject manifests and include time gap, team colour, number posterior and impossible-overlap constraints—an embedding alone must never auto-merge.
- **Pitch:** TVCalib code is MIT, but SoccerNet-trained annotations/weights remain research-only. PnLCalib and No-Bells-Just-Whistles are GPL-2.0. For production, reuse only permissive geometry ideas/code, train the 32-point RF-DETR keypoint model on CC-BY + club-owned frames, and keep venue landmark profiles. SAM2.1 is Apache; SAM3 uses a mutable custom SAM licence and lacks an official Apple backend.
- **Pose:** ViTPose and MMPose/RTMPose/RTMO code/models are Apache-2.0. Sapiens HF cards are CC-BY-NC-4.0 (Sapiens2 uses another custom Meta licence): not production. Ultralytics YOLO-pose and models trained by it are AGPL-3.0 unless an enterprise licence is purchased: not production under the stated policy.

## Dataset fit

- **Production/eval candidate:** SoccerTrack v2 is the only close VEO/BePro-like, panoramic, full-match, pseudonymised dataset found with an explicit commercial-compatible HF licence. It should be the main frozen domain benchmark, while club-owned footage remains the only safe production fine-tuning source.
- **Conditional:** TeamTrack has 4K–8K full-pitch soccer side/top video and 4.37M boxes; repo code is MIT, but a distinct dataset licence was not verified: **dataset licence UNVERIFIED**. Do not train production weights until clarified.
- **Internal eval only:** SportsMOT is CC-BY-NC-4.0. All SoccerNet video/jersey/re-ID/calibration/caption assets are explicitly research-only/NDA and broadcast-domain.
- **Text:** [`Homie0609/MatchTime`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Homie0609/MatchTime) says CC-BY-SA-4.0 (2024-07-02), but it is derived from SoccerNet-Caption, whose source terms say research-only: **do not use for production training**. [`yonaitay/football-scouting-dataset`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yonaitay/football-scouting-dataset) is MIT but synthetic reports generated from names/stats—the opposite of Film Room evidence honesty.

## KILL LIST for production

- **Qwen2.5-VL-3B** — Qwen Research licence explicitly says non-commercial only; a downstream re-labelled fine-tune cannot erase that restriction.
- **Molmo2 / MolmoPoint production checkpoints** — Apache label, but their own cards disclose non-commercial training datasets; internal comparison only pending rights clearance.
- **SoccerNet family (Jersey, ReID, Calibration, Caption, MatchTime-derived)** — SoccerNet’s FAQ says research-only and not intended for commercial purposes; broadcast footage is also the wrong camera domain.
- **SportsMOT** — CC-BY-NC-4.0.
- **Sapiens / Sapiens2** — original HF family is CC-BY-NC-4.0; Sapiens2 is a custom Meta licence, not the requested permissive set.
- **Ultralytics YOLO-pose / YOLO pitch model** — AGPL-3.0 unless separately licensed; train the same CC-BY annotations with Apache RF-DETR Keypoints instead.
- **PnLCalib and NBJW** — GPL-2.0 code is incompatible with this proprietary production policy; their SoccerNet-trained assets also fail the data test.
- **SAM3** — HF licence field `other` / custom SAM licence with unilateral-change language; no official MPS path. SAM2.1 is the clean Apache substitute.
- **DINOv3 re-ID ports** — official DINOv3 HF licence field is `other`, while third-party football/person ports are sparse and often licence-UNVERIFIED; DINOv2 is the clean baseline.
- **Apollo / LLaVA-Video / Gemma 3/4 as evidence models** — generic captions without time+player-region grounding can sound fluent while being unverifiable; okay only as internal caption-style judges, not evidence generators.
- **E-BARD/EBQwen jersey model** — basketball/NBA domain; EBQwen descends from non-commercial Qwen2.5-VL-3B despite its downstream CC-BY label, and source-footage commercial rights are not established.
- **Synthetic “football scouting report” text datasets** — names/stats/template prose train exactly the invented certainty Film Room must avoid.

## Three next experiments (smallest first)

1. On 20 frozen Film Room clips, run Qwen3-VL-8B-4bit with the tracked jersey box overlaid and require `{claim,t0,t1,box,confidence,visibility}`; count only claims whose returned box overlaps the track and compare unsupported-claim rate with the current qwen flow.
2. On SoccerTrack-v2 MOT clips, compare the current merge score against median-of-best-crops OSNet-x0.25 and DINOv2-small embeddings, reporting merge precision first (then recall/IDF1) because a false merge is more dishonest than a missed merge.
3. Convert the 317 CC-BY pitch-keypoint images to RF-DETR Keypoints, fine-tune a small Apache model, and test calibration success by camera class on 50 club-owned panoramic/panning frames before adding SAM2.1 mask refinement.
