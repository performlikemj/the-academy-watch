Ball truth — confirm or correct suggestions

Every visible ball counts. Normal labels default to match ball; review labels default to NOT the match ball. Enter/Space accepts a suggestion; click places a ball; N confirms no ball at all. M toggles match identity. C confirms the existing visible label as-is, NOT the match ball, preserving its coordinates and provenance. Arrows change frame; J/K change clip. Leave uncertain frames unlabelled. Suggestions never save themselves. Export JSONL as a backup.

The key row shows one button per action with its key. Pressing a key or clicking its button does the same thing; the button lights up when that action has saved, and the badge beside it always shows this frame's saved label. Imports always skip rows older than your saved label or clear decision. Restoring a cleared frame requires a newer row and confirmation. Changed confirmed decisions also require confirmation. Cancel keeps storage unchanged; OK applies only eligible rows. Old-page warnings stay until you explicitly Dismiss them. Imports never dismiss a warning. The old-page ghost marker is a guide for manual re-clicking; it never saves a label.

Once v2 browser storage exists, the embedded seed is ignored. Before syncing a kit, export your current browser labels and run compare_label_exports.py against the original export; both steps are mandatory.
